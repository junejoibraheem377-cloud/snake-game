🐍 Snake Arena
A modern, browser-based multiplayer-style Snake game featuring AI-controlled opponents, a dynamic scoring system, speed boosts, and a fully responsive touch-first design — built entirely with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.
🎮 Play it live: https://junejoibraheem377-cloud.github.io/snake-game/
📖 Overview
Snake Arena reimagines the classic Snake game with a Slither.io-inspired experience. Players control a snake in a large open arena, competing against 19 AI-driven bot snakes to grow the longest and highest-scoring snake on the leaderboard — all while avoiding collisions that end the run.
✨ Features
🕹️ Intuitive Touch Controls — Drag your finger anywhere on screen to steer; no on-screen buttons required.
⚡ Speed Boost — Hold to boost your snake's speed at the cost of a small length/score trade-off, adding strategic risk-reward gameplay.
🤖 AI Opponents — 19 independently behaving bot snakes navigate, forage, and collide dynamically within the arena.
🍓 Rich Food Variety — Eight distinct emoji-based food items, each with unique point values, encouraging active foraging.
📊 Live Leaderboard — Real-time ranking of the top 6 snakes by score.
⏸️ Pause & Resume — Full game-state control with dedicated Pause, Resume, and Restart functionality.
📱 Fully Responsive — Automatically adapts to any mobile or desktop screen size.
🎨 Polished Visual Design — Smooth gradients, glowing effects, and an expressive snake design with directional eyes.

🎯 How to Play
Tap Play to enter the arena
Drag your finger (or move your mouse) to steer your snake
Hold your touch to activate a speed boost
Eat food to grow longer and increase your score
Avoid colliding with other snakes' bodies
Use the Pause button anytime to pause, resume, or restart the game
Climb the leaderboard and outlast the competition!
